# Kintsugi ledger: LibreCopy-Grok-Build

Kintsugi mends broken pottery with gold, and the repair is the part people look at. This ledger lists every crack found in the v0 of this repo, the evidence for it, and the seal. A row is `sealed` when a merged change fixes it, with the file that shows the seal, and `hallmarked` once the release installs from GitHub. Open rows are real and wait for a hand; see [CONTRIBUTING.md](./CONTRIBUTING.md#ways-to-contribute).

Grades: **hairline** is copy or cosmetic, **fracture** is wrong behavior with a workaround, **break** means it does not work, loses data, or breaks a safety or privacy promise. IDs are never reused. Evidence line numbers point at v0 (`main` before v1.0.0) unless a row says otherwise.
